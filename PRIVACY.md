# StrumAlong: Privacy & Terms

*Last updated: November 2026*

StrumAlong is a free, open-source play-along app that runs in your web browser. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own.

## YouTube

**Rock Out** songs and the **StrumAlong Listener** use **YouTube API Services** (YouTube's standard embedded video player). When you play a Rock Out song, the video comes straight from YouTube and YouTube handles it, including any ads, cookies and data it collects. StrumAlong never sees your YouTube account.

- By using Rock Out or the Listener you agree to be bound by the **[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)**.
- YouTube's handling of your data is covered by the **[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)**.

## What StrumAlong itself keeps

- **Your settings and songs** (volume, tempo, the song you picked, songs you added or made with the Listener) are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your computer, and clearing your browser data deletes them.
- **The microphone (the Listener and 🎤 Any instrument):** the sound is analysed live to work out chords. **No audio is ever recorded, saved or sent anywhere.** The Listener keeps only a pitch summary in memory, and a finished song holds only chord names and times; Any instrument keeps only the last few seconds of pitch strengths, to judge the chord you just played. The microphone is used only while the Listener is listening or Any instrument is switched on, and stops when you stop it.
- **The guitar:** StrumAlong talks to your AeroBand guitar over Bluetooth on your own computer. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

## What other services see

- **Visit counter:** the hosted site counts visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy), which uses no cookies and keeps no personal data (page, referring site, browser type, screen size and country; IP addresses are not stored).
- **Guitar sounds** load from GitHub Pages and jsDelivr, which see an ordinary web request, like any website.
- **YouTube**, as described above, when a Rock Out video plays.

StrumAlong does not sell, share or collect any personal information, and serves no ads of its own.
