Tips

  • Pick songs with clear guitar or piano chords. Busy, heavily produced mixes are harder.
  • Use the artist's official video or "(Audio)" upload. If a video says it can't play here, try another official upload.
  • The chart only keeps chord names and times. Nothing you hear is saved.
  • Rock songs often use "power chords" with no major or minor: if A and Am both show up, pick the one that sounds right and change the others.
